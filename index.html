Our Story

It started with digging in my bag. We’ve all been there standing in the middle of a crowded room, frantically looking through receipts and old gum wrappers, just to find that one specific lip liner because you just ate and need to touch up. It was really inconvenient and it was NOT aesthetic...

Today, your phone is the one thing that never leaves your hand. It’s your camera, your wallet, your entertainment, and your connection to the world. So I realized.. why aren't your most vital beauty essentials right there with it?

Made from the ambition of a 15-year-old founder, this new product is where aesthetic protection for your phone connects with convenient utility. The precision-engineered molds came from months of prototyping, and they're designed to secure your essential glosses and liners effortlessly. I didn't just want to make a phone case, I wanted a survival kit for anyone on the go.

Today, GlossGard is it's own unique brand. Though fairly new, thanks to all the love and support we've gotten, these cases are helping thousands of people keep their gloss within arm's reach. Because you should be focused on making memories, not digging for your lipliner.

— Samara Sheikh ❤
Founder & CEO
